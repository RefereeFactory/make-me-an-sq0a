How it works

1List what you have going spare — food or goods, with a quantity and a collection point.
2Neighbours browse and claim only what they need. Quantities update automatically for everyone.
3Every listing and claim is saved to permanent cloud storage, so the board stays live across all visitors and visits.
4Organisers unlock the desk with their own code to see claimers and export consented contacts as CSV.

Location search uses The Factory's places service. No card details are ever entered here — any support contributions go through The Factory's secure pay facility.
